# Expense Tracker Launch Plan

Status: no domain, no company, Play Console on a personal account. App name "Ledger" is taken.

## 1. Order of steps

1. **Pick the name, then the domain.**
   - The Android package ID (e.g. `com.name.app`) cannot change after publishing.
   - Do not publish under "Ledger".
2. **Buy the domain and a domain email** (support@, legal@).
   - Google may ask you to verify website ownership in Search Console for an organization account.
3. **Incorporate a company.**
   - Pvt Ltd: best if you may raise money.
   - LLP: cannot issue shares or ESOPs, but lighter and cheaper compliance.
   - Rough yearly upkeep: LLP about ₹10,000–20,000. Pvt Ltd about ₹30,000–50,000.
   - Incorporation gives you PAN and TAN. Then open a current bank account in the company name.
4. **Get a D-U-N-S number (free).**
   - Required for organization accounts.
   - Can take up to 30 days.
   - Indian IDs (PAN, GST, CIN) are not enough for Google.
5. **Move Play Console to the company.**
   - Google now allows converting a personal account to an organization account in place. Apps stay.
   - If conversion is unavailable, create a new organization account and transfer the app.
   - Monetizing personal accounts cannot use the direct owner-transfer process.
6. **Payments must go to the company, not you.**
   - Organization name and address come from the Google Payments profile linked to the account.
   - Create that profile under the company. Payouts go to the company bank account.
   - Do not add paid features or in-app purchases before this is done.
   - Google collects and remits GST on Play sales in India. It requires your PAN.
7. **Apple Developer Program** as an organization.
   - Needs the same D-U-N-S. Fee is $99/year.
8. **Trademark** search and filing (India, Class 9 and 42).
9. **Legal pages:** privacy policy, terms, refund policy, account deletion page. Follow India's DPDP Act.

## 2. Name options (not verified)

Domain availability was not checked. My lookup was blocked. Check each name at a registrar and in Play/App Store search.

- Crowded, avoid: **Tallyo / Tally**, **Kharcha**, **Paisa / Paisly**. Existing expense apps already use these names (Tally, Kharcha, Payly, Kashly, Spendly).
- Candidates to check: **Ledgerly, Outlay, Pennyfold, Spendfold, Cashlane, Tabulo**.
- Pick a name where:
  - `.com` is free (fallback `.app` or `.in`)
  - Play Store and App Store search show no finance app with that name
  - the trademark search shows no conflict

## 3. Low-budget order

Money is limited, so spread the cost:

1. Pick the name and buy only the domain.
2. Apply for the D-U-N-S number (free) as soon as the company exists.
3. Register the company. LLP is the cheaper route. Pvt Ltd if you plan to raise money.
4. Open the company current account.
5. Convert the Play account. The $25 Play fee is already paid.
6. Launch free first. Add paid features only after the payments profile is under the company.
7. Delay the Apple account ($99/yr) and trademark filing until Android earns something.

## 4. Recommended path

Coined name → LLP or Pvt Ltd → D-U-N-S → convert Play account → payouts to the company bank.
