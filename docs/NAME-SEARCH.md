# Name search, 28 Sept 2026

About 450 names checked across five rounds. Domains were checked against the registries' own RDAP services (Verisign for .com, Google Registry for .app, NIXI for .in). "Free" means not registered. A registrar may still price it as premium. Play checks searched the Play Store in India for apps whose title or package ID contains the name.

Not checked: trademarks (IP India's search has a CAPTCHA) and Instagram handles (login wall). Do both before buying.

## Global finalists (the app may go global, so these come first)

A further ~210 English and coined names were checked. Nearly every real English word and short coined name is taken on .com. Play checks here searched both the US and India stores.

### Risks per global finalist

- **Pennykept:** three syllables. India's coin is the paisa, but "penny" is widely understood in Indian English.
- **Inkpenny:** more abstract; could suggest a stationery or tattoo app.
- **Owella:** .com taken; pronunciation may vary ("O-wella").
- **Pennyknot:** .com taken; "knot" can be misread as "not".
- **Trunkbook:** reads like luggage or a car boot.

### Ruled out in the global rounds

- **Khipu and Quipu:** Khipu is a payments company, Quipu is invoicing software.
- **Tieknot and Tabline:** existing Play apps (tie-knot guides, a guitar tab player).
- **Too narrow (IOU-only):** lendback, owedto, ioulog.
- **Look like typos:** kipsake, lentto, iouu.
- **Sound like bill-splitting apps:** evenshare, splitless.
- **US-only reference:** dimebook.
- **Meaningless coinages:** memla, remla, kontli.

### Risks per finalist

- **Haathi:** a common word, so trademark strength is unknown. Some people will spell it "Hathi". "White elephant" is a mild negative.
- **Harpaisa:** reads awkwardly in English ("Harp-aisa"). "Paisa" names are crowded in this category.
- **Chukta:** all three main domains are owned by someone else, possibly for a planned product. Film dialogue also uses "hisaab chukta" to mean settling scores.
- **Potli:** already used by other apps. A "-book" suffix echoes Khatabook.
- **Mohur:** few young users know the word, and the pronunciation is unclear.
- **Gaanth:** also means a lump or tumour, and a grudge ("mann mein gaanth"). Not recommended.

## Eliminated by an existing Play app

- **Baaki:** "Baaki: Expense Tracker Budget" (`com.nainnielabs.baaki`). All baaki- variants are out.
- **Lenden:** LenDenClub (a regulated P2P lender) and "Khata: LenDen Ledger".
- **Udhaar:** 18 apps.
- **Hisaabo:** an expense manager.
- **Bahibook, RokdaBook, SettleBook, Lekha Jokha, RupeeBook, IOUly, Money Keeper.**
- **Cowrie:** Cowries Pay.
- **Owlo:** a scooter company. Owl names also carry "ullu" (fool) in Hindi.
- **Thaili:** Thai Life Insurance.
- **Every rupee:** "BalanceSe - Track Every Rupee".

## Eliminated for other reasons

- **The plan's own candidates:** Ledgerly and Outlay are taken on every ending. Pennyfold, Cashlane and Tabulo are taken on .com. Spendfold and Pennyfold echo Fold Money, an Indian fintech.
- **Chhutta:** also "chhutta saand" (loose bull).
- **Rupeeknot, dueknot:** read as "rupee not" and "due not".
- **Most short English words** (owed, dues, kept, stub, slate, folio, jot, notch, purse, quits, evenly, squared): taken on .com and .app.
