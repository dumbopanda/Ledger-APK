Owner's brief: single words that carry the app's character (cautious, diligent, watchful, precise, keeping track), drawn from many areas and languages. Check the domain and the Play Store.

## What was checked

- **About 950 words** from 20 areas: core traits, watch and record-keeping roles, measuring tools, careful and storing animals, nautical terms, steady nature, Latin, Greek, Hindi and Sanskrit, Japanese, Nordic and German, Romance languages, Arabic and Urdu, Swahili, old English roles, preparedness, money, trail-marking, seeing clearly, and navigation.

- **Prefix and suffix .com** (getword, wordapp, tryword, useword, wordhq, myword, heyword): 120 of the strongest words checked. **99 have a free form.**
- **Play Store:** the same 120 searched in India and the US.

Price for any free .com: about ₹950 a year ($11.08 at Porkbun).
