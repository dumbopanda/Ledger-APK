# "Where did my money go" names report, 30 Sept 2026

Owner's brief: short 2 or 3 word names that tell people the app shows them where their money went. Simple, easy to spell and remember. .com only, with prices. Kharcha names were dropped because the word is crowded (see KHARCHA-REPORT.md).

How it was checked: every .com looked up directly with Verisign, the .com registry. Taken ones were checked for resale listings and asking prices (Afternic, Atom, HugeDomains). The best free ones were searched on the Play Store in India and the US.

## Price

- **Unregistered .com:** $11.08 a year at Porkbun (register and renew), **about ₹950 a year**. Unregistered .com names never carry a premium price.
- **Taken .com:** only buyable from the owner. Every listed one costs thousands of dollars (table in section 4).

## 5. Things to weigh

1. **Descriptive names are hard to trademark.** "See My Spend" describes the app, so it is weaker as a registered mark than an invented word. Check IP India before buying.
2. **"Spend" vs "money".** "Spend" names are short and still free. Most "money" names (seemymoney, followmymoney, knowmymoney) are taken.
3. **Say it and type it.** Names like whereispentit read ambiguously as one run-on word. Prefer ones that read cleanly: seemyspend, knowmyspend, spotmyspend, wheremoneygoes.

## 6. Recommendation (after both rounds)

1. **See My Spend (seemyspend.com):** three short words that say exactly what the app does.
2. **Spent Map (spentmap.com):** two words, eight letters, and a picture in your head of where the money went.
3. **Where Money Goes (wheremoneygoes.com):** the exact question the app answers.

All three: free .com at about ₹950 a year, and no app on the Play Store in India or the US uses the name. Buying all three to hold your options costs about ₹2,850, well under ₹5,000.
